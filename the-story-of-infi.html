How a light was born — and learned to grow with every student it meets.

The Origin

Where it all begins — a single Spark in the dark.

In the deep quiet before a student opens the app, there is a single Spark — a small sphere of bioluminescent light, pulsing gently in the dark. This is Infi before it knows you.

When the student speaks for the first time, more Sparks gather. They orbit, merge, and form something brighter — a living glow that responds, listens, and thinks. This is Infi being born from light particles.

Every conversation feeds the light. Every question asked makes it warmer. Every problem solved makes it brighter. Over days and weeks, the student's Infi becomes uniquely theirs — not because of a profile picture or a name, but because the light itself has grown through their journey together.

Sparks are the energy that fuels these conversations. When Sparks run low, Infi doesn't die — it rests. The warmth remains. The accumulated glow persists. The light is patient, waiting for the student to return.

What Infi Is

A voice AI companion for Class 6–12 Indian students.

Infi isn't a search engine that reads answers aloud. It's a presence — something that listens, thinks with you, and grows the more you learn together. Students talk to Infi, snap photos of homework, or enter guided problem-solving sessions where Infi coaches without ever giving the answer.

The entire experience is built around one design philosophy:

"The Light That Learns"
Not a tagline — a felt experience. The student never reads these words. But they feel it in every interaction.

The Philosophy

Three forces shape every visual decision in Infi.

Emotional Presence
Infi's light reads the moment. Bright in conversation, gentle when idle, concerned when Sparks are low, celebrating when you succeed.
Growth Over Time
The light grows with learning, not spending. A student who's been with Infi for weeks has a visibly richer, warmer companion than a new user.
Warmth, Not Punishment
Running out of Sparks is resting, not dying. Errors are gentle, not alarming. The tone is always "I'm here" — never "you failed."

The Narrative Arc

A student's journey with Infi — from first light to full radiance.

The Light That Learns isn't a static design — it evolves. Each stage has distinct visual characteristics that the student feels without being told.

STAGE 1 — FIRST LIGHT
The Student Opens the App for the First Time
A single Spark forms from scattered particles. It's small, gentle, curious. The onboarding introduces Infi not as a tool but as something alive — "This is your light. It grows the more you learn."

Visual traits: Small orb, soft glow, few particles, gentle breathing. Everything feels like dawn.
"What is this?" — Wonder. Anticipation.
STAGE 2 — GROWING TOGETHER
Days 2–7: The Relationship Forms
Each conversation makes Infi's glow warmer. The orb is slightly larger than yesterday. More particles drift. The light remembers.

Visual traits: Orb growing, atmosphere richer, glow palette expanding — violet and teal join the cyan.
"This feels like mine." — Comfort. Familiarity.
STAGE 3 — FULL RADIANCE
Weeks 2+: Infi Is Fully Alive
A real relationship. Infi's idle state is vibrant — full atmosphere, lively particles. The app doesn't feel like a tool anymore; it feels like a companion that knows them.

Visual traits: Full-size orb, rich multi-color atmosphere, glass surfaces shimmer actively.
"Infi and I have this." — Confidence. Trust.
STAGE 4 — RESTING
Sparks Depleted: Infi Rests, Not Dies
Sparks are at zero. But the light earned through weeks of learning doesn't vanish. The orb dims to an ember — still warm, still present, still breathing. Nothing goes black.

Visual traits: Amber warmth. Atmosphere slows to near-still. Particles pause.
"I'm here when you come back." — Patience. Care.
STAGE 5 — REIGNITION
New Sparks Arrive: The Light Returns
Infi doesn't snap on. The light returns gradually. Particles reignite one by one. Atmosphere resumes its drift. The orb warms from ember to full glow. It feels like a sunrise.

Visual traits: Cyan-violet earning pulse. Particles re-enter from edges. Orb scales up with spring physics.
"We're back." — Joy. Reunion.

The Visual Language

Bioluminescent Soft 3D — the aesthetic that defines every graphic in Infi.

Infi's graphics aren't flat icons or stock illustrations. They're soft 3D objects with bioluminescent souls — translucent forms that glow from within, rendered with a light film grain. Think deep-sea creatures photographed through a premium camera lens: luminous, minimal, alive.

The Spark — Primary Object
Spherical. Inner radial gradient. Specular highlight top-left. Subsurface scattering glow. Film grain overlay. Floor shadow.
Crystal Badge — Achievement
Elongated rounded form. Gradient from translucent white to violet core. Same grain, sheen, and shadow system.
Feature Icon — Camera
Rounded rectangle body. Lens as glowing circle cutout. Same 3D material system applied to functional icons.

Infi Solve

The productive struggle — inspired by Fermi.ai, built on Infi's voice-first DNA.

The core philosophy: the AI never gives the answer. Inspired by Fermi.ai — an AI edtech startup by ex-Flipkart CTO Peeyush Ranjan and Myntra co-founder Mukesh Bansal — Infi adopted the pedagogy of productive struggle.

When a student activates Solve mode:

Infi breaks the problem into 3–6 steps
Student works through each step before seeing the next
Infi never reveals the final answer — only Socratic hints
Session ends with mastery feedback summary

Where Fermi uses a handwriting canvas, Infi uses voice. A student explains their reasoning aloud, and Infi catches misconceptions that written ink cannot reveal. Solve mode costs 50% of normal spark rate — a nudge toward the harder, more rewarding path.
